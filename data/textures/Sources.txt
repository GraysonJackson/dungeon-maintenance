The closed chest is the pushable crate. The pump, valves, part, coal,
heater, furnace, and puddle use the original demo artwork, saved as BMPs.
Magenta (255, 0, 255) is transparent. All sprites load from this folder;
the game does not need a download or an asset conversion step to run.
